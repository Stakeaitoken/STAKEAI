                Frequently asked questions
                
        
                
            
                
                What is STAKEAI?
                
                STAKEAI is an AI-powered crypto staking platform. It offers staking packages, strategy bots, a referral program and a task center in one place.
                
            
                
            
                
                What is crypto staking?
                
                Staking means locking crypto assets in a smart contract for a fixed period. When the term ends, the position can be withdrawn under the contract terms. See our staking guide for details.
                
            
                
            
                
                Is any return guaranteed?
                
                No. Crypto assets involve risk and no return is guaranteed. The calculator on the site is for illustration only.
                
            
                
            
                
                How do I create an account?
                
                Sign up from the top right, click the verification link sent to your email, and log in. We recommend enabling two-factor authentication to secure your account.
