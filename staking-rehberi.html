What is crypto staking? A beginner's guide

Staking means locking crypto assets in a smart contract for a fixed period. This guide summarizes how staking works and what to consider before you start.

How does staking work?

You deposit your assets into the contract for the term you choose. During the term the position stays locked. When the term ends, you can withdraw under the contract terms. Transactions are recorded on the blockchain and can be verified.

Choosing a term

Shorter terms are more flexible, while longer terms keep your funds locked for longer. Choose a term based on when you may need access to your funds.

Risks

  • Crypto asset prices can change quickly.
  • Smart contracts may contain technical bugs.
  • You may not be able to withdraw before the lock period ends.
  • No return is guaranteed.

Security tips

  • Enable two-factor authentication on your account.
  • Never share your wallet password or private key.
  • Only use the official address: www.stakeai.io
